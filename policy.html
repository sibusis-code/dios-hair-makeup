Please Read Before Booking

Booking Policy

At DIOS, we value your appointments and would like to help you make the most suitable reservations with us.

Booking Procedure

  • Finalise your booking directly on our website.
  • A non-refundable minimum 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking for all makeup and installation requests.
  • Your appointment is only secured once the deposit is received.

Rescheduling

  • Rescheduling requests must be made at least 48 hours before your appointment.
  • Your deposit will be transferred to the new date — it will not be forfeited.
  • Failure to give 48 hours' notice will result in loss of your deposit.
  • Appointments may be rescheduled once only.

No Refunds & No-Shows

  • All deposits are strictly non-refundable.
  • If you do not show up for your appointment, your deposit is forfeited in full.
  • In the event of an emergency, please contact us as soon as possible — we handle all situations with discretion and empathy.

Rectifications

  • If you are not satisfied with your service, you must raise it before leaving the studio.
  • We will work with you to correct any concerns at no additional charge.
  • Concerns raised after you have left the premises may not qualify for complimentary rectification.

Have questions about our policy? We're happy to help.
