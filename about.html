Who We Are

Where luxury meets artistry

Our flagship store is in Midrand, with a satellite branch within Copperleaf Estate in Centurion.

At DIOS Hair | Makeup, we are rooted in the spirit of servitude and personalised service, giving each client a tailored beauty experience from consultation to final look.

DIOS means God in Spanish, and that meaning inspires how we serve: with excellence, care and intention. We also place strong emphasis on mobile services, including group work for weddings, celebrations, film productions and corporate shoots.
